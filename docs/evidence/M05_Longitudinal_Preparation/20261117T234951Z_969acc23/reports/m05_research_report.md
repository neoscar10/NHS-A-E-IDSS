# M05 | Longitudinal preparation

## Results
The calendar retains 11,392 observed provider-months and identifies 871 absent records after providers first appear. Of these, 30 fall within observed reporting spans and 841 occur after last observation. No absent counts were set to zero. There are 18 codes with recorded name variants and 0 with parent variants. The latest reporting month (2026-09) contains 120 provisional candidates out of 191 reported providers.

## Method and interpretation
The calendar starts at the first observed month of each code. The candidate rule requires an observed origin and 12 consecutive reported months through that origin, including at least one positive Type 1 observation in the window. It does not use future observations. Recorded activity is a proxy, not verification of service type. Reporting absence is not evidence of closure. Name changes or parent changes are not evidence of mergers; source verification remains necessary. All observations and advisories remain available for modelling design. Publication availability must be addressed before training and evaluation. No forecasting models have been fitted.
